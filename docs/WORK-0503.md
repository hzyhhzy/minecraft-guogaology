## Authorized behavior

Use vanilla `minecraft:is_fall` consistently for manuscript landing protection and Inner-world landing reduction. This includes landing on stalagmite tips, as well as ender-pearl damage wherever vanilla classifies it as falling. Falling stalactites hitting the player are a different damage family and retain ordinary protection.

- Boundary manuscript Lv1: safe distance x2 and fall-family/collision damage x0.5; copies do not multiply this landing effect.
- Boundary manuscript Lv2/3: immune to fall-family damage, even when flight is currently switched off. Lv3 collision immunity stays as before.
- Inner world: safe distance x2 and fall-family/collision damage x0.5; independently multiplies with Lv1's reduction and generic core defense.
- Preserve Underworld landing exemption, native Feather Falling/Protection/Resistance, deferred manuscript editing, foreign/creative flight and the Connector-safe whole-method wrapper.
- Four builds remain supported; actual runtime scope for this patch is native Fabric26.2, native Fabric1.21.1 and NeoForge1.21.1 + Sinytra Connector.

## Connector damage-pipeline correction

The first candidate passed 520 native Fabric1.21.1 assertions but failed the new Boundary I stalagmite assertion on Connector: actual `5.51724` versus expected `2.75862`. Generic core defense worked; the independent landing half was missing. NeoForge's `actuallyHurt` records armor reductions in a DamageContainer and calls `getDamageAfterMagicAbsorb` for its side effects, ignoring its return value. A successful Mixin conversion therefore did not imply successful mitigation.

Move the landing scalar into the existing armor-result wrapper, after native armor and before the native resistance/enchantment stages. NeoForge consumes that return value. Remove the old magic-result RETURN injection so the reduction cannot apply twice. Native fall-tag sources bypass armor; remaining vanilla Resistance/Feather Falling factors remain multiplicative, and absorption still runs afterward. Keep the pre-hit armor-wear context unchanged and add no NeoForge dependency, reflection or loader-specific gameplay branch.

Reference: [NeoForge1.21.1 LivingEntity patch](https://github.com/neoforged/NeoForge/blob/1.21.1/patches/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java.patch). The final candidate passes 544 actual checks on Connector beta.16, including raw ordinary fall, stone-spike and wall reductions in all four worlds. Patch audit: no partial or failed adaptations. The native and26.2 final runs are recorded below.
