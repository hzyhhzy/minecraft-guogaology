# Guogaology 0.4.2 — grounded exits, activated frames and enhancement previews

User request, 2026-10-07: automatically created portal exits repeatedly appear in the sky. Improve site selection. Additional request: every activated frame must have four times obsidian hardness and never drop items, regardless of tool.

## Destination sites

The old search sampled five heights per column. It required all 81 floor cells of a 9×9 footprint to share exactly one height and four completely air-filled layers above. Grass, thin snow and small slopes disqualified ordinary land. Its fallback explicitly preferred a sky candidate at Y≥160, so a valid air platform often won far above accessible terrain.

The replacement keeps the bounded asynchronous 3×3 FULL-chunk preparation and server-only placement. Nearby complete safe gates are still reused first. It scans actual support/clearance transitions down each sampled column, including ground underneath tall foliage, in slices of at most 32 heights per step. The shared 64-step / soft 2ms per-tick search limit, loading feedback, cancellation and deadlines remain.

New exits prefer fully supported level ground. A slope with at most three blocks of height difference can receive short foundations connecting the floor to the ground. Only ordinary replaceable vegetation and thin snow may be cleared: no fluids, containers, cores or solid scenery. Leaves and logs do not qualify as ground. Remaining fallback candidates prioritize ground contact, then water contact, then unsupported air; ties favor less fill. Water platforms sit directly above the surface, without draining the water. No candidate gains priority from being above Y160. Pure void still permits a bounded safe platform instead of hanging or overwriting structures.

Placement is revalidated after the multi-tick search, in case another player changed the candidate. Existing safe exits are not relocated. Changes apply to newly created exits, including in already generated chunks.

## Activated frames

All three destination appearances share `googology:guogao_portal_frame`. Its shared balance-table hardness is now 200, exactly four times vanilla obsidian's 50. Other settings, including blast resistance and harvesting tier, are unchanged. The loot table is empty and the block drop method also returns no items, including Silk Touch and virtual harvesting effects. Breaking a frame retains whole-gate collapse without drops. Unactivated cake/mineral/Guogao materials and the three return-frame items keep their original settings and drops.

## Enhancement and manuscript interface

Unavailable player-inventory items receive a strong gray overlay above their icons/counts; valid items retain the green outline. With no equipment inserted, the table uses its real equipment-slot predicate. With an item inserted, and in a manuscript, it uses the real socket predicates, including compatibility, grade, free capacity and socket Totems. The overlay does not prevent ordinary inventory rearrangement. Updating the equipment or socket contents immediately changes the emphasis.

Both right-side headings say “效果预览 / Effect preview”. Closing still activates manuscript changes; only the redundant in-panel wording has been removed. The panel now describes the edited item's contribution, rather than mixing in incidental held/worn equipment. Native weapon attack remains a separately labelled base value. Normal attack shows independent +HP terms for the manuscript base, Power and Ordinal contributions; deep attack shows their independent ×factors. Weapon Power uses the same realm-sensitive representation, including bows. Added reach, health, regeneration and extra excavation use +, and multiplicative effects use ×. Each armor piece's protection contribution includes its actual slot weight and the confirmed scale of two, ready to sum across armor; the manuscript shows its own protection factor. These are display changes and do not alter the balance or activation/ownership rules.

The input label is right-aligned to x78, leaving a six-pixel gap before the equipment slot at x84. English uses “Manuscript” and a shorter drag hint, keeping the label and hint clear of the actual item and the information panel. Longer targeting/flight labels are shortened so the level or multiplier remains visible.
