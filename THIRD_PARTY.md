# Third-party components and data

- The Gradle Wrapper is distributed under the [Apache License 2.0](licenses/Gradle-Apache-2.0.txt); its upstream copyright/license headers are retained in the launcher scripts. Gradle distributions are downloaded by the Wrapper, not committed here.
- Minecraft, Fabric Loader, Fabric API and build plugins are external build dependencies. Minecraft binaries, libraries, game assets and launcher credentials are not included in this repository.
- The notation catalog was derived from project-provided BMS/iBLP examples and expansion rules. Its metadata records source row conventions and checksums. The source spreadsheet and unrelated personal files are not included; the embedded catalog is sufficient for world generation.
- The logo is drawn from original pixel geometry in `tools/generate_brand.py`; it references the worried/sweating expression and capital Ω without embedding an external emoji image or font asset.
- The mod source retains its existing [MIT license](LICENSE).
